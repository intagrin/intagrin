# Billing FAQ

**"Why was I charged twice?"** Almost always a proration line item from a mid-cycle plan
upgrade/downgrade, not a duplicate charge — pull the specific invoice and check for a line item
labeled "Proration" before assuming it's a billing error.

**Refund policy.** Refunds are approved automatically for amounts of $500 or less. Anything above
that requires a human sign-off before it's issued — never promise a refund above $500 will happen
immediately; tell the customer it needs a quick internal review first.

**"My invoice shows the wrong plan."** Plan changes made near the end of a billing cycle sometimes
appear on the invoice generated *before* the change was applied. Confirm the change's effective
date against the invoice's billing period before treating it as an error.

**Enterprise invoicing.** Enterprise and Enterprise Plus accounts are billed via invoice, not
card-on-file — a "payment failed" report for these accounts usually means their AP team hasn't
processed the invoice yet, not a payment method problem.
